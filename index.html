🐄 Purple Cow Internet

The March to 100,000 Herd Members

An intake form for each team to capture what's working, what isn't, the bottlenecks holding us back, and the innovations that move us forward. Fill it in together — your manager reviews everything in the backend.

🎯 Quarterly goal Quarter
SWOT Start / Stop / Keep Bottlenecks Innovations
How to use this form:
  1. Pick your team and add your name.
  2. Work through all four sections together as a team: SWOT, Start / Stop / Keep, Bottlenecks, and Innovations.
  3. Hit Submit — your manager reviews everything in the backend and decides what to take on this quarter.
Part 1

SWOT — where do we stand?

An honest look at our position. Keep entries short and specific — one idea per line. Add at least 2 items.

Strengths

What we're genuinely good at today that helps grow the herd.

    Weaknesses

    Internal gaps slowing us down or capping how many members we serve well.

      Opportunities

      Outside trends or moves we could seize to accelerate growth.

        Threats

        Competitors, risks, or external forces that could stall the march.

          Part 2

          Start / Stop / Keep

          Concrete actions — what to begin, quit, and protect to free up energy for the march to 100,000. Add at least 2 items.

          Start

          New things worth beginning.

            Stop

            Things draining time with little payoff.

              Keep

              What's working — don't lose it.

                Part 3

                🔧 Bottlenecks

                Things or processes that are holding Purple Cow back — friction, delays, or broken steps we should change. What's slowing us down or capping how well we serve the herd? Add at least 1.

                Part 4

                💡 Innovations

                Ways we should evolve to be a better company — new ideas to grow, delight the herd, and leapfrog the competition. What should we build or become? Add at least 1.

                Ready to submit?

                When your team has filled out all four sections, hit Submit to send it to your manager. Once submitted, your responses are in — you can close the page.

The March to 100,000 Herd Members

An intake form for each team to capture what's working, what isn't, and the priorities for the quarter. Fill it in together — your manager reviews everything in the backend.

🎯 Quarterly goal Quarter
SWOT Start / Stop / Keep Priorities
How to use this form:
  1. Pick your team and add your name — every item you add is tagged with your name.
  2. Work through the three sections together as a team.
  3. Your manager logs in to the backend dashboard to review, sort, add notes, and finalize priorities.
Part 1

SWOT — where do we stand?

An honest look at our position. Keep entries short and specific — one idea per line. Add at least 2 items.

Strengths

What we're genuinely good at today that helps grow the herd.

    Weaknesses

    Internal gaps slowing us down or capping how many members we serve well.

      Opportunities

      Outside trends or moves we could seize to accelerate growth.

        Threats

        Competitors, risks, or external forces that could stall the march.

          Part 2

          Start / Stop / Keep

          Concrete actions — what to begin, quit, and protect to free up energy for the march to 100,000. Add at least 2 items.

          Start

          New things worth beginning.

            Stop

            Things draining time with little payoff.

              Keep

              What's working — don't lose it.

                Part 3

                Priorities — what we'd commit to

                The few things this team believes matter most this quarter. For each one, nail down what "done" looks like in plain, measurable terms. Add at least 1 priority.

                Ready to submit?

                When your team has filled everything out, submit it for your manager to review. You can keep editing and submit again any time.
